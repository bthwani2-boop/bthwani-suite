# 06_SURFACE_CLASSIFICATION

## Required Surfaces

- `app-client`: customer entry, cart, checkout, tracking, and proxy entry
- `app-partner`: partner orders and store readiness
- `app-captain`: offers, execution, and proof capture
- `control-panel`: oversight, exception, peak mode, and proxy review

## Optional Surface

- `app-field`: activation and visit-support only

## Out Surfaces

- `webapp`
- `website`

## Surface Law

- visibility across surfaces does not transfer action ownership
- `control-panel` may observe many families but owns only governance families
- `app-field` may support the service but does not become a default DSH path
