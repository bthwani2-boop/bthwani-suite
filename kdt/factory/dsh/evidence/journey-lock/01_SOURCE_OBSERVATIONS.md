# 01_SOURCE_OBSERVATIONS

## Confirmed Facts

- target `dsh` flow notes already define a lawful baseline sequence from customer entry to partner handling to captain execution to status visibility
- target surface lock and operation surface coverage now make it possible to express that sequence without inventing screens
- donor UX flow keeps the visible lifecycle narrow and the actor responsibilities distinct
- donor traceability gives real evidence for proxy-request and field-support branches while much of the wider donor graph remains unsealed

## Controlled Inferences

- the clean target should model proxy and field as side branches rather than equal peers to the mainline order journey
- returning user handling matters for DSH because tracking and re-entry are common, but it should reuse the same canonical operations instead of creating separate journey ownership
- partner local recovery should precede internal ops escalation whenever the blocker is store-side

## Rejected Carryover

- do not convert donor click metrics into screen contracts at the journey phase
- do not model every donor endpoint as a separate step in the clean journey
- do not place normal partner or captain execution inside `control-panel`

## Remaining Gap

- screen inventory is still required to turn these journey steps into lawful screen candidates without inflating the route tree
